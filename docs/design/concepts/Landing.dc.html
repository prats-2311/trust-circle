How a payment is protected

  1. Step 1 Check the message Paste a text or a screenshot. The AI explains what gave the scam away, in plain words.
  2. Step 2 Pause the payment A risky payment is approved in PayPal but held, so the money doesn't leave and the pressure is off.
  3. Step 3 Ask the real person "Did you just ask Grandma for $800?" One tap from the real Daniel stops it.
  4. Step 4 Warn other families A confirmed scam account is flagged for everyone, and reworded versions of the script are caught too.
